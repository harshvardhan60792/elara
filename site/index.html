Elara

Control your computer with a look, a wave, a word.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

Gestures, actions, and the core UI are built and tested. Voice, presence, and profile auto-switching are still in progress — see the progress log.

What it does

Gestures

Raise a palm and a radial menu appears under your hand. Swipe to change slides. Pinch and lift to turn the volume up. A two-handed frame gesture screenshots exactly the region you outline.

Voice

An offline wake word and a local speech recognizer. Open apps, search, control media, dictate text — nothing is transcribed off-device.

Presence

Elara notices when you leave. It pauses your media, mutes your mic, and resumes when you're back.

Profiles

Bindings switch automatically with the app you're using — slides in PowerPoint, tracks in Spotify, tabs in your browser.

How it works

webcam → MediaPipe hand landmarks → geometric features → pose + motion classifiers → arming gate (confidence, voting, dwell, cooldown) → profile bindings → OS action mic → openWakeWord → Vosk (grammar-constrained) → intent match → same OS actions

Privacy, by construction

  • Runs entirely on your machine. No account, no cloud, no subscription.
  • The webcam opens only while Elara is armed, and closes the moment you disarm it.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting — ever.
  • Every automated test and unattended run defaults to dry-run: it logs what it would do instead of touching your OS.
